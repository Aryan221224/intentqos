How it is wired

Phone (4G) ──USB cable, USB tethering on──▶ this laptop ──Ethernet cable──▶ router's WAN port
  1. Plug the phone into this laptop by USB and turn on USB tethering (Android: Settings › Hotspot & tethering; iPhone: Personal Hotspot).
  2. Press Share the phone's 4G below (Windows asks for permission once).
  3. For the failover test, move the router's WAN cable from the ISP router to this laptop's Ethernet port when the Test Kit asks.
