Find your router

Connect this laptop to the router (Wi-Fi or a LAN port). The router needs its own internet connection, because it downloads Python and tc from its package feed.

Sign in to the router

GL.iNet: the password of the router's admin panel. OpenWrt: the root password you set in LuCI (empty if never set). It is used only to reach the router over SSH and is not stored.

Your router

Your internet plan

From your ISP bill (e.g. 100/100 for a Jio or Airtel 100 Mbps fibre plan). IntentQoS measures the real line too and follows it.

Operator Console

Remove IntentQoS from this router? Shaping stops, and the router's flow offloading and its own QoS settings are put back.
